# Square Camera

A Pixel-style camera app for Android that shoots square (1:1) photos.

- Viewfinder in the photo's shape: **1:1** (default), 4:3 or 16:9 — tap the ratio chip at the top
- Flash (off / auto / on), self-timer (off / 3 s / 10 s), rule-of-thirds grid
- Zoom stops (.5 · 1 · 2 · 5 where the phone has the lenses), pinch to zoom, tap to focus
- Volume keys take a photo
- Photos are saved to DCIM/Camera, so they show up in Google Photos; tap the round thumbnail to open the latest
- Front camera (selfies are saved as previewed)

Settings are remembered between launches.

## Install

Each push builds an APK in GitHub Actions. On your phone, open the repo's **Releases** page, pick
**Square Camera — newest build**, download `SquareCamera.apk`, and allow installing from your browser.
